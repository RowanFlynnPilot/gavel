Village Board Meeting

July 13, 2026
BudgetElectionsDevelopmentOak WiltMunicipal Center Garage

The Kronenwetter Village Board approved an $814,815 bid from SD Ellenbecker for a Municipal Center Garage project, the meeting's most significant financial action, passing unanimously 7-0. The board also voted 7-0 to reinstate a ballot drop box for the August and November 2026 elections, with a directive to track usage and condition data. The board entered and exited closed session regarding redactions to an investigation record before its public records release, but took no action after reconvening.

Discussion

Proposed Revisions to Chapter 41 Elections (CLIPP)

Trustee Coyle moved, seconded by Trustee Lesniak, to adopt Option C in conjunction with holding a public hearing, but the motion was subsequently withdrawn. The board discussed the need for local ordinances, staff changes to address complaints, the three available options, attorney involvement, and outdated language in the Village Code of Ordinances. Staff was directed to address attorney recommendations and return the revisions to the CLIPP Committee for further review.

Kronenwetter Ballot Drop Box Installation

Trustee Stowell moved, seconded by Trustee Lesniak, to reinstate the ballot drop box for public ballot drop-off. The motion was amended to limit the drop box installation to the August and November 2026 elections and to require follow-up reporting on usage numbers and whether the box was used, unused, damaged, or undamaged. The amended motion carried by voice vote 7-0. Discussion covered previous usage and current ballot return requirements and procedures.

Chapter 389 Oak Wilt

The board discussed Chapter 389 Oak Wilt of the Village Code of Ordinances at length, covering oak wilt procedures, past grants, affected residents, neighboring communities' approaches, available oak wilt resources, the role of a tree inspector, and possible suspension of the oak wilt ordinance. No action was taken.

Municipal Center Garage

Trustee Coyle moved, seconded by Trustee Myszka, to approve SD Ellenbecker's bid of $814,815 for the Municipal Center Garage project. The board discussed the building's firewall, the distinction between police and public works portions of the facility, possible future expansion capabilities, and overall costs. The motion carried by roll call vote 7-0.

Closed Session: Personnel Investigation Record Redactions

Trustee Lesniak moved, seconded by Trustee Stowell, to convene into closed session pursuant to Wis. Stat. § 19.85(1)(b) and § 19.85(1)(f) to discuss and take possible action regarding final redactions to an investigation record before its release under the Wisconsin Public Records Law. The motion carried 7-0 and the board entered closed session at 7:07 p.m., reconvening in open session at 7:46 p.m. No action was taken after the closed session.

Action items

  • SD Ellenbecker's bid of $814,815 for the Municipal Center Garage was approved.
  • Ballot drop box approved for reinstatement during the August and November 2026 elections; staff directed to track and report usage, non-usage, and damage data.
  • Chapter 41 Elections revisions referred back to the CLIPP Committee; staff directed to incorporate attorney recommendations before returning revisions.
  • No action taken on Chapter 389 Oak Wilt; discussion only.
  • No action taken following closed session on investigation record redactions.
  • Trustee Coyle requested public comment be added as a future agenda item.
