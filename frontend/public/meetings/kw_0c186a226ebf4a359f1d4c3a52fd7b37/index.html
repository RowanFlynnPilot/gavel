Discussion

Approval of June 29, 2026 Parks Committee Meeting Minutes

Tikalsky moved and Vedvik seconded approval of the June 29, 2026 minutes. The motion carried by voice vote 7-0.

Buska Park Renovation Project Update

Public Works Director Greg Ulman provided an update on park grants and discussed the possibility of integrating donor-name bricks into the Buska Park renovation. No action was taken on this item.

Wausau & Marathon County Parks & Recreation Foundation Fund Grant

Public Works Director Ulman presented information on a grant awarded to the Village and answered budgetary questions from committee members. Tikalsky moved and Myszka seconded a motion to accept a $5,000 local match to the grant. The motion carried by roll call vote 7-0.

Post Village Parks Tour Discussion

Committee members discussed observations from their recent tour of Municipal Park, Towering Pines Park, Buska Park, Friendship Park, and Seville Park. Potential improvements discussed included public restrooms, bench and table additions, shelters, playground equipment updates, ADA compliance, walking paths, community gardens, a skate park, and little libraries. Public Works Director Ulman was directed to compile a list of park improvements and associated costs to present at a future meeting.

August 21, 2026 Concert at Towering Pine Fundraiser Event

The committee discussed potential event details including t-shirts, a musician, routes, entry costs, sponsors, and staff involvement. Tikalsky moved and Lesniak seconded a motion to request up to $2,000 from community events funds to support a fundraiser event. The motion failed by roll call vote 1-6, with only Tikalsky voting in favor.

September 12, 2026 Buska Park 5K Run/Walk Fundraiser Event

The committee discussed event details including sponsors, family attractions, food, donation opportunities, costs, and a musical group. Vedvik moved and Holewinski seconded a motion to request the Village Board approve the 5K Run/Walk as an official Village event. The motion carried by roll call vote 7-0.

Creating a 'Friends of the Kronenwetter Parks' Support Group

No action was taken on this item; it was deferred to the next Parks Committee meeting for discussion.

Action items

  • Village to accept $5,000 local match for the Wausau & Marathon County Parks & Recreation Foundation Fund grant.
  • Public Works Director Greg Ulman directed to create and present a list of park improvements and their estimated costs based on the committee's parks tour observations.
  • Request submitted to Village Board to approve the September 12, 2026 Buska Park 5K Run/Walk as an official Village event.
  • 'Friends of the Kronenwetter Parks' support group discussion deferred to the next meeting.
  • Future agenda items to include possible dog park at Norm Plaza Park, possible disc golf course at the Wellhead land, water access point at Gardner Park or Nelson Roads, bench placement along walking trails, 5K event specifics, and discussion of future Village events.
