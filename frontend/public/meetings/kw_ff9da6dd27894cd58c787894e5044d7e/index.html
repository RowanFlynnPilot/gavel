The Kronenwetter Parks Committee held its August 17, 2026 meeting focused on two major items: developing a 10-year Capital Improvements Program (CIP) for village parks, and planning logistics for the September 12, 2026 Bushka Park 5K Run/Walk fundraiser. The committee used a card-sorting exercise to identify park improvement priorities and tentatively mapped projects across fiscal years 2027–2036, with Bushka Park playground replacement as the top priority for 2027.

Approval of Previous Meeting Minutes

Dan moved to approve the previous meeting minutes as presented; Patty seconded. No discussion followed. The motion passed unanimously on a voice vote.

Village Communications About Parks Within the Community

The committee reviewed the village website's park content, noting Jennifer had added videos in 2023 that were well-received. Members suggested promoting park videos on social media, adding short trail-walking videos, and including a notice in the village newsletter directing residents to check out park resources. Greg noted that once the Chromter Drive project wraps up, the village plans to add wayfinding signs near apartments directing people to trails at the municipal center.

2026–2036 Capital Improvements Program Budget

The committee conducted a card-sorting exercise where each member wrote capital improvement ideas on cards, which were then grouped by park to identify priorities. The top vote-getter was the Bushka Park playground replacement (five or more cards), followed by Friendship/Cercas Park rubber surfacing replacement (estimated $70,000–$80,000), Towering Pines Park shelter and restroom development (proposed as a multi-year civic-style park project), and the municipal center park (restroom and potential disc golf course, estimated $150,000+). The committee tentatively assigned Bushka Park playground for 2027, Friendship Park surfacing for 2029, Towering Pines for 2030–2032, and municipal center park for 2033. General park signage, ADA improvements, and branding were discussed as ongoing priorities, with 2030 floated as an 'ADA year.' Civil Park new playground was placed at the end of the plan, approximately 2036–2037. Greg noted approximately $20,000 remains in a parks fund that could be directed toward near-term improvements such as a trail connecting Friendship Park to the dog park.

September 12, 2026 Bushka Park 5K Run/Walk Fundraiser

Erin Mishka reported $1,000 in sponsorships secured with a goal of $1,225 to fund participant shirts; no registration fee is charged and donations are voluntary. Chief [Cherry McHugh] approved a list of priority intersections for volunteer safety coverage, and committee members offered to staff intersections wearing high-visibility vests. The route was adjusted from the original plan so both the 3K and 5K now start and end at Bushka Park. Relocation (a local business) is hosting a raffle and 50/50 drawing, with the 50/50 beginning the week prior. Water stations will be set up at Bushka Park and civil park (halfway point), with Jennifer volunteering to cover water costs. Start time is 10 a.m. on a Saturday.

Future Fundraising Ideas — Living River Concert

Erin Mishka announced plans for a Living River Concert in May, with $1,000 in sponsorship already secured and an anonymous donor covering the band cost, resulting in no cost to the village. She proposed holding the event at Towering Pines and directing proceeds to the Bushka Park Fund Reserve. A committee member (Tim) raised a constituent concern about why residents must fundraise for parks rather than relying on tax dollars; other members acknowledged the concern but noted the village board has been supportive, including a $5,000 match on a recent grant. A running total of approximately $16,000 raised for Bushka Park was referenced.

Quick Fixes and Small Improvements

Members identified several non-capital quick fixes for parks crew attention: removing no-dog signs, repairing a broken plexiglass circle on old wooden playground equipment at Bushka, adding paper towel holders in restrooms, installing at least one handicap swing, adding a bike rack on the farmers market side of Bushka Park, refreshing old wooden park signs at Nor-a-Plaza, Friendship, and Gooding parks, adding sand and a net to the Savil Park volleyball court, and investigating bike route signage connectivity with Rothschild. Greg noted the parks crew has been pressure-washing slides, shelters, and equipment during the slow grass-cutting period.

Next Meeting Date

The committee discussed transitioning from bi-weekly to monthly or as-needed meetings, noting that Dan's return to teaching makes the 4 p.m. time slot difficult. After discussion, the group tentatively agreed to meet Tuesday, September 8 (pending confirmation that a quorum can attend), at 4:30 p.m., with the primary focus of reviewing CIP dollar amounts to present to the village board and finalizing 5K run details.
