Minutes from July 13, 2026 Planning Commission Meeting

A commissioner moved to approve the minutes and received a second. The motion carried without discussion or opposition.

Public Hearing – Rezoning of 4205 and 4307 Weston Avenue (B2 to LI) and Conditional Use Permit for Personal Storage Facility

Staff presenter Jennifer explained that both the comprehensive plan (updated 2024) and the Weston Avenue corridor plan (2022) designate this area for commercial development with B2 and B3 as preferred zoning districts, and that staff recommends against the rezoning to LI. Applicant Steve (last name not fully stated, address 8303 Alderson Street West) argued the corridor has seen no commercial development in the years since the plan was adopted and that the property has had no serious commercial interest in 15 years of his ownership. His engineer Nick from Marathon Technical Services explained the applicant chose LI over B3 due to less stringent landscaping and building material requirements. Dean Dietrich, attorney for the Wausau Child Care Foundation (owner of the adjoining parcel to the east), appeared to note the foundation had no formal position but was interested as an adjoining landowner, and indicated the foundation board would likely be open to selling the property if no major change in childcare funding occurs. Commissioner Joel noted he personally had little objection to storage use given the lack of retail development and proximity to existing LI-zoned land, while staff emphasized the employment objectives of the corridor plan and noted the commission has seen four similar storage facility applications in recent years with mixed results. The commission worked through four determination questions and found the rezoning inconsistent with the comprehensive plan on questions one, two, and three, with a split (approximately 3-2) on question four regarding consistency with surrounding land uses given the adjacent LI zoning. The commission voted 3-2 to recommend denial of the rezoning to the village board.

Motion to Defer Action on Conditional Use Permit – 4205 and 4307 Weston Avenue

Because the property is not currently zoned to allow a personal storage facility, the commission voted to defer action on the conditional use permit pending the village board's decision on the rezoning request. The motion carried without opposition.

Special Exception Sign Permit – LNS Electric, 5707 Scoffeld Avenue

Staff presented a request from LNS Electric to reface an existing freestanding monument sign and add a decorative top element. The proposed sign would be 9 feet tall with an 8-foot setback from the property line; village code requires a setback equal to sign height, triggering the special exception. Staff recommended approval. A commissioner moved to approve and the motion carried unanimously without significant debate.

Animal Fancier License – 5512 North Birch Lane (Lenzo/Bukowski)

The applicant Kira Lenzo (formerly Bukowski) requested a license to keep four dogs for sled dog activities. The village humane officer recommended denial based on insufficient property size relative to the number and size of the dogs. Three public commenters spoke: neighbor Barbara Schultz (1304 [inaudible] Street) supported the license, saying the dogs are not noisy; neighbor Ceda (5604 [inaudible] Lane) opposed it, citing odor from dog waste on a patio area draining toward her yard and an unresolved conflict with the applicant; and Nancy Vet (2306 North 4th Avenue, Wausau), grandmother of the opposing neighbor's children, also opposed, citing smell and the small size of the residence. The commission sided with the humane officer's findings and voted unanimously to recommend denial to the village board.

Planning and Development Department Monthly Project Update

Jennifer reported on three conditional use permits with compliance concerns to be brought before the commission next month: You Sam at 3107 Concord Avenue (vehicles parked in a required green space buffer), the Bug property (a 2022 project with expired building permits and no construction progress), and Tommy's Car Wash (failure to supply annual tank inspection reports and liability insurance documentation as required by their conditional use permit). She also noted salt shed inspections have begun with some corrections needed. The commission acknowledged the report and expressed support for continuing it.
