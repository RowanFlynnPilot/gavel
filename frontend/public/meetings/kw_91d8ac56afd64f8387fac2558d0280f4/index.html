Discussion

Approval of July 27, 2026 meeting minutes

Vice President Rick Smith moved, seconded by Commissioner Donna Rule, to approve the July 27, 2026 meeting minutes with a correction noting that Vice President Smith arrived late rather than being absent. The motion carried 5-0 by voice vote.

Acceptance of Mason Hoffmann and Jarret Imlach as full members of the Kronenwetter Fire Department

Vice President Smith moved to accept the Fire Chief's recommendation to elevate Mason Hoffmann and Jarret Imlach to full membership in the Kronenwetter Fire Department following completion of their probationary periods. Commissioner Lucene Udulutch seconded the motion, which carried 5-0 by voice vote.

Closed session and action on personnel complaint

Commissioner Rule moved and Vice President Smith seconded a motion to convene into closed session under Wis. Stat. § 19.85(1)(f) to address a personnel matter. The commission entered closed session at 5:05 p.m. and reconvened in open session at 6:50 p.m. Following closed session, Vice President Smith moved to continue investigating the complaint dated June 30, 2026, seconded by Commissioner Udulutch; the motion carried 5-0 by voice vote.

Action items

  • Mason Hoffmann and Jarret Imlach are to be accepted as full members of the Kronenwetter Fire Department upon completion of their respective probationary periods.
  • The commission directed that the investigation into the complaint dated June 30, 2026 continue.
