The Board was expected to review and potentially approve a list of legal firms used by the District for various legal needs. The presentation was estimated to take 1 minute.

Set Annual Meeting Date and Location

The Committee of the Whole was expected to recommend a date, time, and location for the budget hearing and annual meeting to ensure compliance with statutory requirements (s.120.08(1)(a)) for an annual meeting between May 15 and October 31. This procedural item was estimated to take 1 minute.

Share Equalized Value Estimates

The District was scheduled to present projected property value changes, with an estimated increase of 4.03% for the 2026-27 mill rate calculation purposes. Since certified property value would not be available until October 1, the projected value was expected to be used for the initial budget and during the annual meeting.

Recommendation for 2026-2027 Tax Levy

The Board was expected to consider the tax levy corresponding to the 2026-27 budget for presentation at the Budget Hearing and Annual Meeting. This presentation was estimated to take 2 minutes.
