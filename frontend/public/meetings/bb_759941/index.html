Discussion

Legal Expenses Summary for 2025-2026

Dr. Elizabeth Channel, Assistant Superintendent of Operations, was scheduled to present a summary report of all legal counsel expenses incurred during 2025-2026, separated by law firm and by type of legal advice sought. This was expected to be a written report requiring no action, with an estimated presentation time of one minute.

Approve List of Legal Firms

The Committee was expected to consider a summary list of legal firms used by the District for various necessary legal needs. Action was requested to approve the list for the 2026-2027 fiscal year, with an estimated presentation time of one minute.

Set Annual Meeting Date and Location

Administration was expected to request that the Committee recommend to the Board a date, time, and location for the budget hearing and annual meeting to ensure compliance with Wisconsin statute (s.120.08(1)(a)) requiring the annual meeting to occur between May 15 and October 31. This recommendation would allow the full Board to approve the details in a timely manner so that all statutory notices and timelines could be met.

Share Equalized Value Estimates

The Committee was scheduled to receive information on projected property value changes for the 2026-27 mill rate calculation. Since certified property values would not be available until October 1, the District was expected to present a projection assuming uniform property value changes across municipalities, with the District's property value estimated to increase 4.03% for budget and annual meeting purposes.

Recommendation for 2026-2027 Budget

Dr. Elizabeth Channel was scheduled to present the 2026-27 Budget Booklet for initial review and was expected to request that the Committee recommend the preliminary budget for use at the Budget Hearing and Annual Meeting. The presentation was estimated to take 15 minutes.

Recommendation for 2026-2027 Tax Levy

The Committee was expected to consider the tax levy contained in the 2026-27 Budget Booklet to support the budget. Once approved, this levy was expected to be presented at the Budget Hearing and Annual Meeting, with an estimated discussion time of 10 minutes.
