Discussion

July 20, 2026 Parks Committee Meeting Minutes

Tikalsky moved, Vedvik seconded, to approve the July 20 meeting minutes with a correction noting that Tim Strachota had been listed as Dan Strachota. The motion carried by voice vote 6-0. Clerk Jennifer Poyer was tasked with making the name correction.

Village Communications About Our Parks Within the Community

The committee discussed current park information on the village website and social media channels and exchanged ideas for better highlighting the parks. No formal vote was taken. Committee members were tasked with reviewing the website and making recommendations for changes.

Additional Ideas for Other Parkland and Related Village Property

Tim Strachota presented ideas for park changes, and the committee discussed a wide range of topics including a potential dog park at Norm Plaza Park, a disc golf course on Well-head Park land, water access points, trail benches, Gooding Park, Triangle Park, grants, and funding sources. The committee also discussed security at the well-head and Filtration Facility and how to align improvements with the Village's Outdoor Recreational Plan. Members were tasked with compiling a quick-fix list and identifying their three top park priorities.

2027-2036 Capital Improvements Program (CIP) Budget – 1st Draft as of 07/31/2026

Finance Director/Treasurer John Jacobs presented the first draft of the Capital Improvements Program, explaining its purpose and how park plans fit within the program. Staff answered committee questions regarding financing the Buska Park remodel. No formal vote was taken.

September 12, 2026 Buska Park 5K Run/Walk Fundraiser Event

Tikalsky moved, Vedvik seconded, to table discussion of the Buska Park 5K Run/Walk Fundraiser until the next meeting. The motion carried by voice vote 6-0. Administrator James Davel presented concerns about the event including staffing and other unresolved details.

Creating a 'Friends of the Kronenwetter Parks' Support Group

Tikalsky moved, Vedvik seconded, to form a Friends of Parks group within the village. The motion carried by voice vote 6-0. The committee discussed the advantages and financing of such a group, and members were tasked with finding volunteers to participate.
