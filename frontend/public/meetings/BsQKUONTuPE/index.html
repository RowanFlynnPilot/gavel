Discussion

Approval of minutes

A motion was made and seconded to approve the minutes of the preceding meeting. The motion passed on a voice vote with no discussion.

Sanitary sewer capacity assessment and flow monitoring study – vendor selection

Staff reported that three proposals were received from HDR, Clark Dietz, and Strand, and that four staff members evaluated them. Scores were described as close but Clark Dietz scored highest. A motion was made by a board member to approve Clark Dietz for the study, seconded, and approved unanimously on a voice vote with no further debate.

Pay Estimate #33 – Lead service line replacement (Community Infrastructure Partners)

A pay estimate of $602,642.35 was presented for lead service line replacement construction work. Staff noted the project remains on schedule and is expected to close the following day, August 26th. The motion to approve was made, seconded, and passed unanimously on a voice vote.

Pay Estimate #5 – Stark Street, 2026 Street Construction Project A (Switlick and Sons)

A pay estimate of $596,518.20 from Switlick and Sons for the Stark Street portion of the 2026 street construction project was presented. A motion to approve was made and seconded; the item passed on a voice vote with no discussion.

Portland cement concrete license – Antler Concrete

A license application and bond from Antler Concrete for Portland cement concrete work was reviewed. A board member indicated they had reviewed the materials and moved to approve the application and bond; the motion was seconded and passed unanimously on a voice vote.

Action items

  • Clark Dietz selected and approved to conduct the sanitary sewer capacity assessment and flow monitoring study
  • Pay Estimate #33 for $602,642.35 to Community Infrastructure Partners for lead service line replacement approved
  • Pay Estimate #5 for $596,518.20 to Switlick and Sons for Stark Street (2026 Street Construction Project A) approved
  • Portland cement concrete license and bond for Antler Concrete approved
