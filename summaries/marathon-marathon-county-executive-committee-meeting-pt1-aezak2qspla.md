## Meeting Overview
The Marathon County Executive Committee met to discuss the Hub City Wind Farm alternative energy advisory committee report, 2027 budget development, and an employee appreciation day proposal, before moving into closed session regarding contractual issues with broadband provider Bug Tussle/Hilbert Communications. The most significant discussion centered on the wind farm report, with committee members debating whether to pursue a Joint Development Agreement with Alliant Energy or oppose the project, ultimately directing administration to compile a pros-and-cons analysis of all available options for a future meeting. The committee also voted to enter closed session to address an approximately $18 million construction lien filed against Bug Tussle's parent company Hilbert Communications.

## Key Discussions
### Public Comment – Hub City Wind Farm
Three residents spoke against the proposed Hub City Wind Farm. Joe Bergen, town chairperson for the Town of Day, urged the board not to enter a JDA with Alliant Energy, citing incomplete environmental studies, the unprecedented height of the proposed 680-foot turbines, and a contact with the Town of Saratoga (Wood County) whose chair reportedly said they 'wish they never signed a JDA.' Cindy Nelson of the Town of Rib Falls described her home as being surrounded by three proposed turbines and said the JDA would put her health at risk, noting a nearby high-density residential area. Heidi Pesky Donna McMillan praised the advisory committee's work, specifically thanking Supervisors Chris Dickinson and Dwayne Gao, and urged the board to honor the committee's recommendation not to pursue a JDA.

### Approval of June 11th Meeting Minutes
The minutes were moved by Supervisor Morash and seconded by Supervisor Arstead with no discussion. The motion passed unanimously.

### 5A1 - 2027 Annual Budget Development and Policy Recommendations
Administrator Leonard briefly outlined the budget development process, noting work on standardizing fees related to the tax foreclosure process. Supervisor Robinson explained the goal of engaging county board members and standing committees early in budget development, with the Human Resources Finance and Property Committee examining a broader county fee policy. No votes were taken; the discussion was informational.

### 5A2 - Marathon County Board and Employee Appreciation Day at the Fair
Administrator Leonard presented a proposal to replace the traditional employee appreciation banquet with an event at the county fair during daytime weekend hours, noting similar approaches used by other counties. He stated he was not requesting a budget amendment and brought it forward for transparency. The committee raised no objections, and Leonard noted unanimous consent in support of moving forward.

### 5A3 - Hub City Wind Farm Report and Next Steps
Supervisor Robinson presented the alternative energy advisory committee report, summarizing findings including recommended increased setbacks, stronger decommissioning obligations, baseline environmental studies, community benefit funds, and infrastructure cost coverage. The committee's recommendation was strongly against pursuing a JDA. Extensive debate followed: Chair Gibbs suggested keeping JDA discussions open to understand what concessions could be extracted; Supervisor Dickinson cited St. Croix County's experience where Excel Energy withdrew from JDA negotiations and opposed a JDA, calling instead for coordination with the Wisconsin Counties Association and PSC; Supervisor Morash urged listening to constituents and researching the health and safety statute angle given the unprecedented turbine heights; Supervisor Hummer expressed sympathy for renewable energy and concern that not pursuing a JDA would leave the county without a seat at the table; Supervisor Boots raised questions about financial and legal exposure if the PSC overrules the county; an unnamed supervisor who served on the committee stated he had entered it intending to support a JDA but reversed position after attending all public hearings. No vote was taken; Supervisor Robinson moved to bring the matter back at the next meeting with administration tasked to prepare a comparative analysis of JDA, intervention, health and safety statute tools, and outreach to St. Croix County.

### Closed Session – Bug Tussle/Hilbert Communications Contracts and Litigation
Corporation counsel explained that Bug Tussle (parent company Hilbert Communications), the county's broadband infrastructure contractor, had indicated its goals would not be realized, triggering contractual implications. Additionally, Michael's Underground Cable Inc. had filed a construction lien of nearly $18 million against Hilbert Communications for unpaid work, accompanied by a companion lawsuit. The committee voted by roll call to enter closed session to discuss legal strategy.

## Public Comment
Three speakers addressed the committee. Joe Bergen, town chairperson of the Town of Day, urged the board not to pursue a JDA with Alliant Energy, citing incomplete environmental studies, unprecedented turbine heights, and a negative report from the Town of Saratoga's experience with a JDA. Cindy Nelson of the Town of Rib Falls said her home is surrounded by three proposed turbines and that a JDA would endanger her health and that of nearby residents in a high-density area. Heidi Pesky Donna McMillan thanked the advisory committee, specifically Supervisors Dickinson and Gao, and urged the board to follow the committee's recommendation against a JDA, listing specific environmental and land-use factors the PSC would scrutinize.
