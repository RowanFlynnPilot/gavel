## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Police and Fire Commission held a brief public meeting on July 27th before moving into two closed sessions: one to interview two firefighter EMT candidates for the Wausau Fire Department, and another to strategize recruitment and appointment of a new deputy police chief. The meeting was largely procedural, with no public comment offered and routine approval of the prior meeting's minutes.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of June 22nd Meeting Minutes
The chair asked if there were any corrections to the June 22nd minutes. Hearing none, a motion was made and seconded to approve. The motion passed unanimously on a voice vote.

### Closed Session – Firefighter EMT Candidate Interviews
Commissioner Cheryl moved to enter closed session under Wisconsin State Statutes section 19.85 for the purpose of interviewing two candidates for firefighter EMT positions with the Wausau Fire Department. The motion was seconded and passed on a unanimous roll call vote with all five commissioners voting in favor.

### Closed Session – Deputy Police Chief Recruitment Strategy
Immediately following, a second closed session motion was made under Wisconsin State Statutes section 19.85 to strategize the recruitment and appointment of a new deputy police chief and to discuss the hiring process. The motion was seconded and passed unanimously on roll call, with all five commissioners voting in favor.

## Public Comment
No public comment was offered. The chair noted no one in the room wished to speak.
