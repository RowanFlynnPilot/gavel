## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Board of Public Works met on June 30th and approved a series of contractor pay estimates, change orders, and a new project bid award in a brief, consent-heavy session. Key decisions included awarding the 2016 downtown sidewalk improvement project to low bidder Pember for $105,799.75, approving a $136,795 change order for additional demolition work at the water treatment plant, and a conditional approval of a pay estimate for the Washington Street Siphon Replacement Project pending clarification on whether a formal change order is required by the DNR.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of Minutes – 6/23/2026
Minutes from the previous meeting were moved, seconded, and approved unanimously with no discussion.

### 2016 Downtown Sidewalk Improvement Project – Bid Award
Two bids were received: Pember at $105,799.75 and Norcon at $127,301. The board moved to award to low bidder Pember and approved unanimously.

### 2026 Street Construction Project A (Stark Street) – Switlick & Sons Change Order No. 1
Staff explained the change order was needed to establish a pay item for 15-inch storm sewer, replacing 12-inch pipe at one location (approximately 40 feet) and making tie-ins at two additional locations (11 ft and 5 ft). The total change order amount is $5,040. Approved unanimously.

### 2026 Street Construction Project A – Switlick & Sons Pay Estimate No. 2
Pay estimate of $434,994.08 was verified by project inspector Kurt Summerlot and project engineer T.J. Approved unanimously without discussion.

### 2026 Pavement Marking Project – Brick Line Pay Estimate No. 1
Pay estimate of $105,342.65, verified by sign shop staff, was approved unanimously without discussion.

### 2025 Street Construction Project B (Fulton, First, Second Streets) – Switlick & Sons Final Payment
Final payment of $8,490.74 was verified complete by T.J. and approved unanimously without discussion.

### Washington Street Siphon Replacement – A-1 Excavating Change Order No. 4 and Time Extension
Staff explained the project is complete but final closeout is pending with the DNR due to federal funding involvement. The change order covers replacement of an electrical box discovered under removed sidewalk, totaling $2,903.25. A time extension was also requested, moving the deadline from February 20th to September 1st. Approved unanimously.

### Washington Street Siphon Replacement – A-1 Excavating Pay Estimate No. 3
A board member questioned a negative balance shown on the pay estimate. T.J. explained the project went over budget due to rock excavation exceeding the bid quantity, triggering a supplemental bid item that was not included in the original purchase order. T.J. confirmed the rock excavation costs are covered under DNR funding. The board approved the pay estimate contingent on verification of whether a formal change order is required by the DNR for the supplemental rock excavation costs. The motion was amended to include this condition before passing unanimously.

### 2025 Water Treatment Plant Demolition – MRD Group Change Order No. 2
Staff reported that during demolition, two additional buildings were found to have footings and footing walls below the groundwater table. After consultation with staff from community development and finance, the decision was made to remove the foundation footings (unlike the deep underground clearwell, which was left in place) to facilitate potential future development. The change order totals $136,795. Finance confirmed budget was available as the overall project came in under budget. Approved unanimously.
