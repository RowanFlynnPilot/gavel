## Meeting Overview
The Kronenwetter Police and Fire Commission voted unanimously to accept Mason Hoffmann and Jarret Imlach as full members of the Kronenwetter Fire Department upon completion of their probationary periods. The commission also spent approximately 105 minutes in closed session deliberating on a personnel complaint, emerging to vote 5-0 to continue investigating a complaint dated June 30, 2026. Both substantive actions passed 5-0.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of July 27, 2026 Meeting Minutes
Vice President Rick Smith moved, seconded by Commissioner Donna Rule, to approve the July 27, 2026 meeting minutes with a correction noting that Vice President Smith arrived late rather than being absent. The motion carried 5-0 by voice vote.

### Full Membership of Mason Hoffmann and Jarret Imlach in the Kronenwetter Fire Department
Vice President Smith moved, seconded by Commissioner Lucene Udulutch, to accept the Fire Chief's recommendation to elevate Mason Hoffmann and Jarret Imlach to full membership in the Kronenwetter Fire Department upon completion of their probationary periods. The motion carried 5-0 by voice vote. No public comment was recorded on this item.

### Closed Session and Action on Personnel Complaint
Commissioner Donna Rule moved, seconded by Vice President Smith, to convene into closed session under Wis. Stat. § 19.85(1)(f) to address personnel matters that, if discussed publicly, could substantially harm the reputation of individuals involved. The commission entered closed session at 5:05 p.m. and reconvened in open session at 6:50 p.m. Following the closed session, Vice President Smith moved, seconded by Commissioner Udulutch, to continue investigating the complaint dated June 30, 2026; the motion carried 5-0 by voice vote.

## Public Comment
No public comment was recorded.

## Action Items
- Mason Hoffmann and Jarret Imlach are to be accepted as full members of the Kronenwetter Fire Department upon completion of their respective probationary periods.
- The commission directed that the investigation into the personnel complaint dated June 30, 2026 continue.
