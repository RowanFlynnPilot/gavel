## Meeting Overview
The Marathon County Board of Supervisors held its adjourned organizational meeting on July 28, 2026, approving several budget amendments and resolutions including state Food Share funding, District Attorney contingency funds, and Sheriff's Office emergency management grants totaling $64,568. A consent agenda item (13A1) was pulled and referred back to the Human Resources Finance and Property Committee after Supervisor Robinson raised questions about it. The meeting was brief, with all contested items passing and the board adjourning after announcements about the Wisconsin Valley Fair and a planned county government day at the fair.

## Key Discussions
### Standing Committee Reports and Consent Agenda Preparation
Supervisor Robinson reported that the Human Resources Finance and Property Committee received its annual audit report from John Ryder of Baker Tilly, who noted continued improvement in financial preparation quality. Robinson thanked Finance Director Samantha Fensky and her department. He also requested that item 13A1 be pulled from the consent agenda and referred back to committee due to unresolved questions; Board Chair Gibbs confirmed no objection from the board and the item was removed.

### Consent Agenda Items C8 through C13B (excluding 13A1)
Supervisor Hopper raised a question about item 12A3, a rezone for Sandra Trembling, noting that a checkbox on page 13 of the paperwork appeared unchecked. The ERC committee chair responded that it was a typographical error and that the applicant had in fact provided the required information reviewed by the committee. The consent agenda passed unanimously.

### Resolution 40-26: Food Share Quality Assurance Reform Mandates Budget Amendment
The Health and Human Services Committee and Human Resources Finance and Property Committee jointly brought forward this budget amendment to receive state funding for Food Share Quality Assurance Reform Mandates. The resolution required a two-thirds majority vote as a budget amendment. It was moved by Supervisor Gaul and seconded by Supervisor Greiner, and it passed but was not unanimous.

### Resolution 41-26: District Attorney Non-Lapsing Contingency Funds
Moved by Supervisor Arstead and seconded by Supervisor Coil, this resolution approving use of District Attorney non-lapsing contingency funds passed unanimously. Supervisor Greiner cast a verbal 'aye' which the chair noted for the record.

### Resolution 42-26: Wisconsin Emergency Management Grants for Sheriff's Office
This resolution approved Wisconsin Emergency Management grants for the Sheriff's Office and amended the 2026 budget by $64,568. Moved by Supervisor Pool and seconded by Supervisor Gao, it required a two-thirds majority as a budget amendment and passed unanimously.

### Resolution 45-26: 2027 Budget Priorities and Guidelines
Moved by Supervisor Mask and seconded by Supervisor Robinson, this resolution establishing the 2027 budget priorities and guidelines passed but was not unanimous, indicating some dissent among supervisors regarding the upcoming budget direction.

## Public Comment
No public comment was offered.
