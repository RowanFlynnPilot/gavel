## Meeting Overview
The Village of Kronenwetter Police and Fire Commission voted 4-0 to continue investigating a complaint dated June 30, 2026, following a closed session that lasted nearly an hour and 45 minutes. The commission also approved revisions to the Citizen Charge Form and accompanying Citizen Charge Procedures, subject to the Village Attorney's review. Both actions were taken unanimously by the four commissioners present.

## Key Discussions
### Closed Session - Personnel/Disciplinary Matters
Commissioner Rule moved and Commissioner Udulutch seconded a motion to convene into closed session under Section 19.85(1)(f), regarding financial, medical, social, personal histories, or disciplinary data of specific persons. The motion carried 3-0 by roll call vote, with the session beginning at 5:03 p.m. Vice President Rick Smith arrived during the closed session at 5:06 p.m. The commission reconvened in open session at 6:46 p.m. on a motion by Commissioner Udulutch, seconded by Commissioner Rule, carried 4-0.

### Action After Closed Session - Complaint dated 06/30/2026
Vice President Smith moved, seconded by Commissioner Udulutch, to continue investigating the complaint dated June 30, 2026. No additional details about the nature of the complaint or the subject were disclosed in the open session record. The motion carried 4-0 by voice vote.

### Approval of July 13, 2026 Meeting Minutes
Vice President Smith moved and Commissioner Rule seconded approval of the July 13, 2026 meeting minutes as written. The motion carried 4-0 by voice vote.

### Citizen Charge Form and Procedures
Vice President Smith moved to approve changes made to the Citizen Charge Form as submitted, conditioned on review and approval by the Village Attorney for both the Citizen Charge Form and the Citizen Charge Procedures. Commissioner Rule seconded the motion. The motion carried 4-0 by voice vote.
