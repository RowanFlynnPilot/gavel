## Meeting Overview
The Marathon County Public Safety Committee met on July 7, 2026, approving two resolutions: one authorizing use of district attorney non-lapsing contingency funds for a $10,000 comfort room project to improve victim privacy, and another accepting a budget amendment for approximately $64,568 in Wisconsin emergency management grants for sheriff's office robotics and squad team equipment. The meeting also featured a recognition of Bob Pound for nearly 40 years of service to the county's Local Emergency Planning Committee and an extensive 2027 budget development discussion covering rising costs, fee structures, and potential service delivery changes.

## Key Discussions
### Recognition of Bob Pound
Emergency Management Director Phil Rent Maester presented a formal recognition to Bob Pound, a founding member of the Local Emergency Planning Committee since its inception in 1987, who officially retired last month. The statement honored his nearly four decades of service and his background as a long-term educator in the fire service. Pound declined to speak at length when invited, drawing laughter from the committee.

### 2027 Annual Budget Development Discussion
County Administrator Leonard and a committee chair identified as Robinson led an extensive discussion on the 2027 budget process, emphasizing three key questions: what services to deliver, at what service level, and at what cost. Notable concerns raised included a 25% projected increase in IT equipment costs, rising road maintenance costs tied to oil prices, and the long-term challenge of funding an estimated $56-57 million highway facility project against $30 million in reserves. Supervisor Brandon raised the idea of in-house fleet upfitting for the sheriff's department as a cost-saving measure, which Leonard indicated staff was already exploring. Committee member John raised expanding the Crisis Assessment Response Team beyond its current 8-to-5 weekday hours to evenings and weekends given the volume of mental health calls to law enforcement. Supervisor Tim requested future reports on jail fee collection and a comparative update on diversion and treatment court success rates.

### Resolution: DA Non-Lapsing Contingency Funds for Comfort Room Project
The committee reviewed a proposal to use approximately $10,000 from the district attorney's non-lapsing contingency fund to create a separate entrance and hallway for a victim comfort room, improving privacy and reducing risk of victims encountering defendants or their families. District Attorney Mayo was not present; Administrator Leonard explained and endorsed the request, noting it was a logical and reasonable use of the non-lapsing fund mechanism the board created last year. The motion was made by Deb and seconded by Brandon, passing without stated opposition and forwarded to the HR Finance and Property Committee.

### Budget Amendment: Wisconsin Emergency Management Grants
A representative presented a budget amendment to accept two emergency management grants totaling $64,568 awarded to the sheriff's office for robotics and squad team equipment purchases. The short turnaround on the grants prevented earlier committee action. Jen moved the motion and Deb seconded; the committee approved forwarding to the finance committee.

### Year-to-Date Budget Report
Finance staff member Sam presented first-quarter budget reports through March 2026 for all public safety departments, including clerk of courts, district attorney, emergency management, medical examiner, and sheriff's office. All departments were at or below the personnel spending benchmark of 23.75%. Emergency management's lower apparent percentage (10.79%) was explained by the practice of charging wages to grants first. Administrator Leonard noted the sheriff's office budget is the county's most levy-dependent and is budgeted tightly, with no flexibility to delay emergency response overtime costs, but expressed no deep concern about any department's financial position.

### Future Agenda Items
Chair Robinson requested a future agenda item to familiarize the committee with the park deputies program. Supervisor Bill raised the question of pedestrian and bicycle safety on Highway X at Camp Phillips in the Village of Kronenwetter, referencing state Safe Routes to School grant funding and proposing a potential path from Howland Avenue to Evergreen Elementary on Tower Road. The chair indicated the matter would be referred to Chair Gibbs to determine whether it falls under public safety or infrastructure committee jurisdiction.

## Public Comment
One public comment was offered by the committee chair, directed to a county employee named Candace who was apparently departing for a new career. The chair offered brief farewell remarks expressing appreciation for her service and wishing her well. No members of the public offered comment.
