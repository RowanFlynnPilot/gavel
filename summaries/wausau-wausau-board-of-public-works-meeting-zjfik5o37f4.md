## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Board of Public Works held a brief meeting on July 21st, approving several financial items related to the lead service line replacement project, site investigations, and infrastructure work. The board tabled the City Hall and Fire Department concrete project bids for further review, while unanimously approving six other action items totaling over $2.2 million in expenditures.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of July 14th Minutes
A board member moved to approve the July 14th minutes and another seconded. The motion passed unanimously with no discussion.

### Item A: 2026 City Hall and Fire Department Concrete Project Bids
Three bids were received: Concrete Creations at $145,846 (low bid), Norcon Corporation at $152,436.70, and Eight Pine Incorporated at $160,587.61. Staff noted there are multiple funding sources involved and indicated the board would take the bids back for review and bring the matter back the following week. No vote was taken on awarding the contract.

### Item B: Change Order No. 7 — Lead Service Line Replacement (Five Star Energy Services)
Change Order No. 7 represents a deduct of $252,712 to the contract with Five Star Energy Services, primarily due to a reduction in the number of homes with rock encountered during construction, along with quantity updates. A board member moved to approve and another seconded; the motion passed unanimously.

### Item C: Pay Estimate No. 30 — Lead Service Line Replacement
The board considered pay estimate number 30 with Community Infrastructure Partners in the amount of $2,188,222.64, covering construction, engineering, and miscellaneous project costs. A member moved to approve, another seconded, and the motion passed unanimously with no debate.

### Item D: Pay Estimate — Outreach Grant Program (June Work)
A payment of $22,622.19 was presented for work completed in June under an outreach grant from the DNR, which staff noted helps offset costs deemed ineligible under the main DNR funding. The board approved the payment unanimously without discussion.

### Item E: Amendment to REI Agreement — Thomas Street Corridor Area A Site Investigation
The amendment covers additional testing required by the DNR under the site investigation work plan, totaling $26,375 with no advance payment. Staff noted the majority of the increase — nearly $24,000 — is for lab analysis and additional borings. The board approved unanimously after brief clarifying questions about the cost breakdown.

### Item F: Amendment to REI Agreement — 2026 Riverfront Parking Lot
Staff explained that multiple unforeseen design revisions were required, including relocating the underground stormwater detention basin, raising the parking lot grade by approximately six inches, and adjusting lot grades accordingly — all generating additional work for REI. The board approved the $1,300 amendment unanimously, with a staff member expressing support for the additional compensation.

### Item G: 2026 Asphalt Patching Project — Pay Estimate No. 3
Pay estimate number 3 with Zillah Asphalt in the amount of $1,750.47 was presented, with staff noting quantities had been reviewed and approved by the project inspector. The board approved unanimously with no discussion.

## Public Comment
No public comment was offered.
