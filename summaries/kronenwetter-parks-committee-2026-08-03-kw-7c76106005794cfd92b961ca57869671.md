## Meeting Overview
The Village of Kronenwetter Parks Committee held its August 3rd meeting to discuss village communications about parks, additional ideas for park improvements including a second dog park, disc golf course, water access points, and trail benches, as well as reviewing a first draft of a 2026-2036 Capital Improvements Program budget. The committee reached broad consensus to delay the September 12 Bush Cook Park fun run/walk fundraiser due to inadequate planning, and agreed to each bring their top three park priorities to the next meeting to help focus the committee's direction and budget requests.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of July 20th Meeting Minutes
The chair noted one name correction had already been provided to staff. A motion was made by Patty and seconded by Elizabeth to approve the minutes with that correction. There was no further discussion and the motion carried 6-0.

### Village Communications About Parks
Chair Mishka raised the item after a Facebook post showed a resident was unaware of public park amenities near Municipal Center Park. The committee reviewed the village website live and found outdated information, including a listing for volleyball courts where no net exists. Members suggested linking existing YouTube park videos directly to each park's page, adding maps of walking trails, and including information about the Kronenwetter and Leather Camp forest management units. Village Administrator Jim suggested a 'one park a month' social media highlight campaign beginning in spring 2027, tying park communications to the budget process to keep residents informed of planned enhancements.

### Additional Ideas for Other Park Land and Related Village Property
Committee member Tim presented several ideas: a second dog park at Norm Klaas Park (already listed as medium priority in the capital improvements plan), a disc golf course at the 33-acre Well Head Park (with security camera concerns raised for the wellhead facility), water access/boat launch points at Gardner Park and the end of Nelson Road, and benches along walking trails potentially funded through memorial donations or Eagle Scout projects. The Village Administrator urged the committee to prioritize all ideas rather than presenting them piecemeal to the board, emphasizing laser focus on Bush Cook Park for 2027 and recommending members each bring their personal top three major priorities to the next meeting. Staff noted the 2025 Outdoor Recreation Plan already includes prioritized projects with cost estimates that should serve as a starting point.

### 2026-2036 Capital Improvements Program Budget First Draft
Village Finance Director John Jacobs presented the first-ever long-range CIP document for the village, which lists projects across all departments including every park. He explained that only 2027 items will be formally adopted by the board in November, while outer years remain a living planning document. Jacobs noted the village currently has roughly 80% of its borrowing capacity available and discussed debt structuring strategies to smooth tax impacts. The $200,000 listed for Bush Cook Park in 2027 is expected to come from donations, and the Administrator stressed the committee must make a strong case to the board to supplement any funding gap. Members were encouraged to share the CIP document with neighbors to build community support.

### September 12, 2026 Bush Cook Park 5K Run/Walk Fundraiser Event
Village Administrator Jim raised significant concerns about the September 12th date, noting police officers are scheduled a year in advance and would require overtime, a large portion of fire volunteers would be unavailable that weekend due to commitments in the Fox Valley, and no detailed operational plan had been presented to the committee. A member also noted September 12th is adjacent to the 9/11 anniversary, which felt inappropriate for a fundraising event. The committee broadly agreed the event should be delayed, with the Administrator planning to contact Trustee Aaron Myszka, who had organized the effort, to discuss postponing to spring 2027, potentially tying a fun run to a ribbon-cutting celebration if the playground equipment is funded and installed.

## Public Comment
No public comment was offered.
