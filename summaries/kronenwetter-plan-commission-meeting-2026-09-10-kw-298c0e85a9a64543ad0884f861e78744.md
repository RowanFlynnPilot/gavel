## Meeting Overview
Based on the published agenda, the Village of Kronenwetter Plan Commission was scheduled to hold a public hearing and consider three significant development and zoning matters, including a conditional use permit and site plan for a Marathon County highway facility, a zoning change request for residential development on Lea Road, and a proposed text amendment to allow zero-lot-line homes in the village.

## Key Discussions
### Conditional Use Permit - Marathon County Facility
Troy Torgerson was scheduled to request a Conditional Use Permit for a Highway Facility on approximately 25.2 acres at 2384 & 2388 Old Hwy 51, Kronenwetter. The Commission was set to review the permit request and hear public testimony before taking action on the application.

### Site Plan - Marathon County Facility
A site plan for the Marathon County Highway Facility at 2384 & 2388 Old Hwy 51 was scheduled for review and discussion. The Commission was expected to consider the facility's site plan design and layout in connection with the conditional use permit request.

### Zoning Change - Cywinski 1900 & 1904 Lea Road
William Cywinski was scheduled to request a zoning change of approximately 26.7 acres from Agriculture and Residential (AR) to Rural Residential 5 (RR5) at 1900 & 1904 Lea Road. The Commission was set to consider the rezoning request and receive public input before determining appropriate action.

### Certified Survey Map - Cywinski 1900 & 1904 Lea Road
A Certified Survey Map (CSM) request was scheduled for the Cywinski property to create two lots and one out lot from the existing two-lot configuration totaling 33 acres. The Commission was expected to review the land division proposal in conjunction with the zoning change request.

### Text Amendment of Chapter 520 - Zero-Lot-Line Homes
The Village of Kronenwetter was scheduled to present proposed amendments to Chapter 520 (Zoning) to allow zero-lot-line residences. The amendments were set to modify subsections 520-20 and 520-138 and related figures, with a draft ordinance available for public review at the Municipal Center.
