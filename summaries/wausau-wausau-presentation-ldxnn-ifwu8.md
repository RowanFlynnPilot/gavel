### Retirement Presentation for Sheryl Bahr
Mayor Diny provided historical context about Wausau in 1998 when Bahr joined the Police and Fire Commission. Police Chief Matt Barnes spoke, noting that every sworn member of the Wausau Police Department — including himself — had been interviewed by Bahr, and praised her ability to put candidates at ease. The fire chief also spoke, noting that Bahr had participated in interviews for nearly every member of the fire department as well as all promotions in both departments, emphasizing her attentiveness and follow-up questions. Bahr received a certificate of recognition and a plaque and offered brief remarks praising Wausau as 'one of the greatest cities in the United States' and lauding the integrity of the police and fire departments.

### Certificate Presentation
Mayor Diny read aloud the certificate: 'City of Wausau, Wisconsin recognizes Cheryl Bar in recognition and appreciation of your leadership, insight, and dedication as a commissioner of the Wausau Police and Fire Commission from 1998 to 2026.'
