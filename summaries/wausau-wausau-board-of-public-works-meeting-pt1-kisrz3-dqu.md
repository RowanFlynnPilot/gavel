## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Board of Public Works held a brief regular meeting on August 12th, approving three contractor pay estimates and a concrete license before moving into closed session to deliberate on claims. All items passed unanimously with minimal discussion.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of August 4th Meeting Minutes
A board member moved to approve the minutes of the August 4th regular Board of Public Works meeting, which was seconded and passed unanimously with no discussion.

### 2026 Asphalt Paving Project — ARC Pavers Pay Estimate No. 3
The board considered Pay Estimate No. 3 from ARC Pavers in the amount of $322,718.16 for the 2026 asphalt paving project. A motion to approve was made and seconded, passing unanimously with no additional discussion.

### Downtown Utility Project — Haas Sons Incorporated Pay Estimate No. 3
Pay Estimate No. 3 from Haas Sons Incorporated in the amount of $136,498.03 for the downtown utility project was brought before the board. A motion to approve was made and seconded, passing unanimously without debate.

### Portland Cement Concrete License — Ross Construction
A board member stated they had reviewed the Portland cement concrete license application from Ross Construction and moved to approve it. The motion was seconded and passed unanimously.

### Closed Session to Deliberate on Claims
The board voted unanimously to enter closed session pursuant to Wisconsin Statute 19.85(1)(g) for the purpose of deliberating on claims. No details about the nature of the claims were disclosed in open session.
