## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Finance Committee met on July 14, 2026, and took action on several significant items including approving a new Human Resources Coordinator position funded from reserves (3-2 vote), ratifying a two-year collective bargaining agreement with transit union Local 1168, approving a grant-funded firefighter paramedic apprenticeship program with North Central Technical College, and approving a sales study for signal reconstruction projects at Sturgeon Eddy Road and Grand Avenue intersections before adjourning to closed session to deliberate on property parcel payments.

## Key Discussions
### April 2026 General Fund Financial Report Review
Finance staff member Maryanne presented the April 2026 general fund report, noting it was a holdover from the prior meeting due to a printing error. Key concerns flagged included a fuel escalator clause in the refuse/recycling contract generating approximately $10,000 in additional monthly charges due to high diesel prices (noted at over $4.60/gallon), delayed motorpool charges from heavy March snowfall not yet reflected in the report, and CCITC software renewal expenses front-loaded in Q1. On the positive side, municipal court fees increased for the first time since 2013, and the SAFER grant received an extension beyond its February 2026 expiration. Alder Watson asked about miscellaneous revenue variances and the timing of snow removal costs; no formal action was required or taken.

### Approving Human Resources Coordinator Position and Related Budget Modification
Outgoing HR Director Noak addressed the committee, clarifying that she had been planning her departure since April and brought this position forward deliberately to benefit her successor. Alder Neil made a motion to approve the position as soon as possible, with funding from reserves as outlined in the resolution. Alder Watson voted no, arguing the decision should be weighed against other staffing priorities in the full 2027 budget process. Alder Tierney initially questioned the timing given the director's departure but ultimately seconded the motion after hearing Noak's rationale. The motion passed 3-2, with Alder Watson and Alder Hanki voting no.

### Approving Bargaining Agreement with Local 1168 Amalgamated Transit Union AFL-CIO
HR Director Noak summarized the two-year agreement, noting a 2% wage increase for the first six months followed by 1.5% increases for the remaining contract periods through June 30, 2028. Additional changes included expanded uniform allowances for safety shoes and weather gear, along with language cleanup. Noak described the negotiation as smooth, completed in three sessions. The motion was made by Alder Watson and seconded by Alder Tierney; it passed unanimously with no notable opposition.

### Approving Firefighter Paramedic Apprenticeship Partnership with North Central Technical College
Fire Chief [name referred to as 'Chief Cop' by chair] gave an extensive presentation on the program, detailing a severe recruiting crisis that saw vacancies rise from 2 in 2021 to 14 in 2024 before briefly dropping to one. The grant-funded 18-month program would accept two cohorts of nine apprentices at wages starting at $14/hour, covering hiring costs estimated at over $30,000 per traditional hire, equipment costs over $143,000, and FTO incentive pay estimated at $52,000. Apprentices would be limited-term employees and could not accrue overtime. Alder Tierney confirmed FTO pay would not generate overtime. Alder Hanki raised concerns about workers' compensation risk from inexperienced apprentices; the chief and HR director responded that apprentices would not be deployed in hazardous roles and that ergonomic and wellness supports were already in place. The motion was made by Alder Watson and seconded by Alder Tierney; it passed unanimously.

### Approving Sales Study for Business 51 / County Highway N / Sturgeon Eddy Road Project
Eric Lynman introduced the sales study covering four project IDs related to traffic signal reconstruction at the Sturgeon Eddy Road and Townline Road/Grand Avenue intersections, a state-funded project with a 90-10 grant split. The study determines property values for temporary limited easements and fee acquisitions needed before 2026 construction. Alder Neil asked whether a delayed pedestrian crossing green light feature could be incorporated; Lynman said he would raise it with the DOT. The motion was approved unanimously with no significant opposition.

### Approving Nominal Payment Parcel Report and Motion to Adjourn to Closed Session
The committee moved to closed session under Wisconsin Statute 19.85(1)(e) to deliberate on parcel payments for seven properties along Sturgeon Eddy Road and Grand Avenue related to the Business 51/County Highway N project. Alder Hanki made the motion to adjourn to closed session; Alder Watson seconded. A roll call vote was taken and all five members voted in favor. The chair noted the committee would return to open session only if action needed to be reported publicly.

## Public Comment
No public comment was offered. The chair asked twice if anyone was present for public comment and, receiving no response, dispensed with the public comment statement.

## Action Items
- Human Resources Coordinator position approved; to be funded from reserves per budget modification; job listing to be held until a new HR director is hired
- Two-year collective bargaining agreement with Local 1168 Amalgamated Transit Union ratified for July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2028
- Firefighter paramedic apprenticeship partnership with North Central Technical College approved; grant-funded program to begin with first cohort of nine apprentices for 18 months
- Sales study for Business 51/County Highway N/Sturgeon Eddy Road signal reconstruction project approved, enabling property acquisition offers to proceed
- Committee adjourned to closed session to deliberate on nominal payment parcel report for seven properties along Sturgeon Eddy Road and Grand Avenue
- City engineer to inquire with WisDOT about feasibility of delayed pedestrian crossing green at Townline Road intersection
