## Meeting Overview
The Village of Kronenwetter's Administrative Policy Committee approved $37,884 for bike path sealing out of general obligation funds and an accompanying budget adjustment splitting costs between the capital projects fund ($20,954) and TID #2 fund ($16,930), both passing 5-0. The committee also approved a $14,815 budget adjustment for the Municipal Garage capital improvements fund. All substantive motions carried unanimously.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of June 15, 2026 Meeting Minutes
The committee approved the June 15, 2026 Administrative Policy Committee meeting minutes as presented. The motion was made by Solheim and seconded by Kluz, passing on a voice vote of 4-0 with Fredel, Solheim, Kluz, Stowell, and Swanson voting yea.

### Budget Adjustment #3 – Municipal Garage
The committee approved a budget adjustment directing an additional $14,815 into the capital improvements fund for the Municipal Garage. The motion was made by Fredel and seconded by Solheim, passing on a roll call vote of 5-0 with Fredel, Solheim, Kluz, Stowell, and Swanson voting yea.

### 6/30/2026 Financial Statements (Budget Status Report)
The June 30, 2026 financial statements and budget status report were discussed. No action was taken on this item.

### Bike Path Sealing
The committee approved a recommendation to fund $37,884 in bike path sealing out of general obligation funds. The motion was made by Fredel and seconded by Kluz, passing on a roll call vote of 5-0 with Fredel, Solheim, Kluz, Stowell, and Swanson voting yea.

### Budget Adjustment #4 – Bike Path Sealing
The committee approved a recommendation to the Village Board for 2026 budget amendments covering the bike path sealing project, allocating $20,954 to the capital projects fund and $16,930 to the TID #2 fund. The motion was made by Solheim and seconded by Kluz, passing on a roll call vote of 5-0 with Fredel, Solheim, Kluz, Stowell, and Swanson voting yea.
