## Meeting Overview
The Village of Kronenwetter's Administrative Policy Committee approved $37,884 for bike path sealing and an accompanying budget adjustment splitting costs between the capital projects fund and TID #2, both on 5-0 votes. The committee also approved a $14,815 budget adjustment for the Municipal Garage capital improvements fund. All substantive motions carried unanimously.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of June 15, 2026 Meeting Minutes
The committee voted to approve the June 15, 2026 Administrative Policy Committee meeting minutes as presented. The motion was made by Solheim and seconded by Kluz, passing on a voice vote of 4-0 with Fredel, Solheim, Kluz, Stowell, and Swanson voting yea.

### Budget Adjustment #3 – Municipal Garage
The committee approved Budget Adjustment #3, directing an additional $14,815 into the capital improvements fund for the Municipal Garage. The motion was made by Fredel and seconded by Solheim, passing on a roll call vote of 5-0 with Fredel, Solheim, Kluz, Stowell, and Swanson voting yea.

### 6/30/2026 Financial Statements (Budget Status Report)
The June 30, 2026 financial statements and budget status report were presented for discussion. No action was taken on this item.

### Bike Path Sealing
The committee voted to recommend approval of $37,884 drawn from the general obligation fund for bike path sealing. The motion was made by Fredel and seconded by Kluz, passing on a roll call vote of 5-0 with Fredel, Solheim, Kluz, Stowell, and Swanson voting yea.

### Budget Adjustment #4 – Bike Path Sealing
The committee recommended to the Village Board that 2026 budget amendments be made to the capital projects fund and TID #2 fund for the bike path sealing project, in the amounts of $20,954 and $16,930 respectively. The motion was made by Solheim and seconded by Kluz, passing on a roll call vote of 5-0 with Fredel, Solheim, Kluz, Stowell, and Swanson voting yea.

## Public Comment
