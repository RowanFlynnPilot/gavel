## Meeting Overview
The Kronenwetter Police and Fire Commission voted 4-0 to advance Kristy Isberner in the firefighter/first responder hiring process following a closed session in which Fire Department applicants were interviewed. The commission also voted 4-0 to seek additional information regarding a personnel incident documented as of June 30, 2026, and tabled updates to its Citizen Charge Procedures and Form until the next meeting after a motion to approve changes was withdrawn.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of June 22, 2026 Minutes
Vice President Smith moved, seconded by Commissioner Rucinski, to approve the June 22, 2026 meeting minutes as submitted. The motion carried 5-0 by voice vote.

### Closed Session — Fire Department Applicant Interviews and Personnel Matters
Commissioner Udulutch moved, seconded by Vice President Smith, to convene into closed session under Wis. Stat. 19.85(1)(c) and 19.85(1)(f) to interview Fire Department applicants and consider personnel matters. The motion carried 5-0 by roll call vote and the commission convened at 5:03 p.m. with Chief O'Brien invited to participate. Commissioner Udulutch departed the meeting at 6:30 p.m. The commission reconvened in open session at 7:05 p.m. on a motion by Vice President Smith, seconded by Commissioner Rucinski, which carried 4-0.

### Action After Closed Session — Firefighter/First Responder Hiring
Commissioner Rucinski moved, seconded by Commissioner Rule, to advance Kristy Isberner in the firefighter/first responder hiring process. The motion carried 4-0 by roll call vote.

### Action After Closed Session — Personnel Incident
President Vedvik moved to seek additional information regarding the incident. Vice President Smith requested the phrase 'we received June 30, 2026' be added to the end of the motion; President Vedvik accepted the amendment. Commissioner Rule seconded the amended motion, which carried 4-0 by roll call vote.

### Citizen Charge Form and Procedures
Vice President Smith moved to approve suggested updates to the Citizen Charge Procedures and Citizen Charge Form as discussed, but withdrew the motion after further discussion indicated additional changes were needed. President Vedvik then moved to delay action to the next meeting, seconded by Vice President Smith; the motion carried 4-0 by voice vote.

## Public Comment
No public comment was recorded.

## Action Items
- Kristy Isberner is to be advanced to the next stage of the firefighter/first responder hiring process.
- Commission staff or administration to gather additional information regarding the personnel incident received June 30, 2026, for review at a future meeting.
- Action on updates to the Citizen Charge Procedures and Citizen Charge Form delayed to the next commission meeting.
