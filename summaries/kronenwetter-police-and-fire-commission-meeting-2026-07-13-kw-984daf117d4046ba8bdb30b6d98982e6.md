## Meeting Overview
The Kronenwetter Police and Fire Commission voted 4-0 to advance firefighter/first responder applicant Kristy Isberner in the hiring process following a closed session that included interviews of Fire Department applicants. The commission also voted 4-0 to delay action on updates to its Citizen Charge Procedures and Citizen Charge Form until the next meeting after a motion to approve changes was withdrawn.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of June 22, 2026 Meeting Minutes
Vice President Rick Smith moved and Commissioner Chet Rucinski seconded approval of the June 22, 2026 minutes as submitted. The motion carried 5-0 by voice vote.

### Closed Session – Fire Department Applicant Interview and Personnel Matters
Commissioner Lucene Udulutch moved and Vice President Smith seconded a motion to convene into closed session under Wis. Stat. 19.85(1)(c) and 19.85(1)(f) to interview Fire Department applicants and consider personnel matters. The motion carried 5-0 by roll call vote. The commission entered closed session at 5:03 p.m. with Chief O'Brien invited to participate. Commissioner Udulutch departed the meeting at 6:30 p.m., and the commission reconvened in open session at 7:05 p.m. on a 4-0 roll call vote.

### Action After Closed Session – Firefighter/First Responder Hiring
Commissioner Rucinski moved and Commissioner Donna Rule seconded a motion to advance Kristy Isberner in the firefighter/first responder hiring process. The motion carried 4-0 by roll call vote.

### Action After Closed Session – Seek Additional Information Regarding Incident
President Vedvik moved to seek additional information regarding the incident; Vice President Smith successfully requested that the phrase 'we received June 30, 2026' be added to the end of the motion, which President Vedvik accepted as an amendment. Commissioner Rule seconded the amended motion, which carried 4-0 by roll call vote.

### Citizen Charge Form and Procedures Review
Vice President Smith moved to approve suggested changes to the Citizen Charge Procedures and Citizen Charge Form as discussed, but withdrew the motion following further discussion about additional changes. President Vedvik then moved to delay action to the next meeting; Vice President Smith seconded, and the motion carried 4-0 by voice vote.

## Public Comment
No public comment was recorded.

## Action Items
- Kristy Isberner is to be moved forward in the firefighter/first responder hiring process.
- Staff or commission to seek additional information regarding the incident received June 30, 2026.
- Action on updates to the Citizen Charge Procedures and Citizen Charge Form deferred to the next commission meeting.
