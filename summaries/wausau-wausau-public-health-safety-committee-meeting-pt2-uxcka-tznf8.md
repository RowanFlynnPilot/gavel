## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Public Health and Safety Committee held a brief second session following a closed session deliberation on a rent abatement case. The committee voted unanimously to abate rent by 20% of monthly rent for a tenant at a property with building code violations, covering the period from April 29, 2026 through August 13, 2026. The decision provides retroactive compensation to the tenant for the diminished value of the rented premises due to the landlord's continued code violations.

## Key Discussions
### Rent Abatement — Building Code Violation Property
Following a closed session, Alder Lukens moved to abate rent by 20% of monthly rent retroactive to April 29, 2026 (the date of the initial inspection) through August 13, 2026 (the date the Wausau Inspection and Zoning Division verified the building code violation was corrected). The chair read the formal determination stating the abatement was intended to provide 'fair and equitable compensation to the tenant for the diminished value of the rented premises due to the landlord's continued violation of the City of Wausau's building code.' The motion passed unanimously 4-0 on roll call vote.

### Adjournment
Alder Watson moved to adjourn and Alder Trueblood seconded. The motion passed unanimously and the meeting was closed.

## Public Comment
No public comment was offered.

## Action Items
- Rent abatement of 20% of monthly rent to be applied retroactively from April 29, 2026 through August 13, 2026 for the affected tenant
- Wausau Inspection and Zoning Division verification of building code correction on August 13, 2026 serves as the end date for the abatement period
