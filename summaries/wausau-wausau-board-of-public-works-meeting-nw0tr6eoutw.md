## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Board of Public Works held a brief meeting on August 18, 2026, approving several pay estimates and a change order related to ongoing infrastructure projects including lead service line replacement and the Washington Street Retaining Wall. The board also acknowledged receipt of three RFPs for a sanitary sewer capacity assessment and flow monitoring study, with staff directed to review and return a recommendation.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of Minutes — August 12, 2026
A board member moved to approve the minutes of the preceding meeting and a second was offered. The motion passed unanimously on a voice vote with no discussion.

### 2A: RFP for Sanitary Sewer Capacity Assessment and Flow Monitoring
The presiding officer reported that three RFPs were received from HDR, Strand Associates, and Clark Dietz. No formal action was taken; the board acknowledged receipt and directed staff to review the proposals and return a recommendation at a future meeting.

### 2B: Pay Estimate Number 32 — Community Infrastructure Partners, Lead Service Line Replacement — $1,349,998.30
The presiding officer noted that Director Lindman had confirmed the work was already performed and recommended approval. A motion was made and seconded; the board approved the payment unanimously on a voice vote.

### 2C: Pay Estimate — Community Infrastructure Partners, Outreach Grants Program (July) — $24,411.14
A board member moved to approve the pay estimate for outreach grants program work completed in July; the motion was seconded and passed unanimously with no notable discussion.

### 2D: Washington Street Retaining Wall — Yankee General Contractors Change Order Number One — $2,917.89
Staff member T.J. Niksich explained the change order covers the cost of raising utility structures to final grade that were intentionally left low during the adjacent lift station project to allow vehicle traffic, and for which no bid item existed in the Washington Street contract. The board approved the change order unanimously on a voice vote.

### 2E: Washington Street Retaining Wall — Yankee General Contractors Pay Estimate Number Three — $72,187.25
The presiding officer noted the pay estimate had been reviewed by T.J. Niksich. A motion to approve was made and seconded; the board approved unanimously on a voice vote with no additional discussion.

No public comment was offered.
