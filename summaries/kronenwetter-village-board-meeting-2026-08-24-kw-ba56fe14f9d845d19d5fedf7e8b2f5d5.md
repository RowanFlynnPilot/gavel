## Meeting Overview
The Kronenwetter Village Board held its August 24, 2026 regular meeting, covering a range of financial and operational matters including a 10-year capital improvement plan presentation, TID #1 valuation challenges, and a collective bargaining negotiation update. The board convened into closed session to address a police union contract and directed the administrator to continue negotiations before adjourning.

## Key Discussions
### Public Comments
One speaker, Mr. Cramer, addressed the board regarding a previous special meeting where a trustee took over the chairmanship, calling it unusual and raising accountability concerns. He also cited the village attorney's budget memo noting the village is $1,000 over budget after seven months due to personnel investigation costs, and criticized the ongoing investigation timeline as several months with no public answers, stating 'I think you have a major accountability problem.'

### Administrative Staff Report
The administrator reported on a meeting with the Golden Pond HOA regarding pond level management, noting the village found seven homes still running sump pumps when the HOA claimed only two, and that the village declined to raise the pond level as a result. The administrator also flagged a significant increase in open records requests targeting the police department, particularly records from when Village President Joling was police chief, characterizing them as a personal vendetta. Staff discussed potentially charging for staff time associated with open records requests and updating public comment procedures.

### Public Works Director Report
Public Works Director Greg reported that a WPS transformer installed for a new electrical panel was the wrong wattage, causing a delay of a few days on Lift Station 2. He also noted that a road contractor is working on drainage and shoulder improvements near the municipal building and bridge areas. A generator battery failure was discovered and addressed in-house, with discussion about the aging generator fleet and the need for a long-term replacement plan.

### Finance Director Report - General Fund and TID #1
Finance Director John Jacobs reported the general fund revenues are tracking nearly identically to 2025 at about 62.7% collected through seven months, with most remaining revenue expected from the state in October. Three expenditure areas flagged as over budget - legal/investigation costs, municipal building operations, and garbage/recycling - are expected to be addressed via a transfer from the $60,000 contingency reserve later in the year. On TID #1, Jacobs explained a combined $2.5 million valuation reduction resulted from local assessor adjustments and a retroactive state correction, worsening the TID's cash flow. He recommended a status quo approach for six to nine months to allow a potential distribution center project to add tax increment, noting debt payments drop significantly starting in 2030 and TID #1 debt is eliminated by 2037-2044.

### 2027-2036 Capital Improvement Plan
Finance Director Jacobs and Public Works Director Greg presented the 10-year CIP, noting the village currently has roughly $49 million in remaining borrowing capacity at 70% of the debt limit. The first-year 2027 program totals approximately $4.3 million across equipment, infrastructure, parks, and facilities. Trustee Erin Mishka (Parks Committee Chair) specifically noted that the $200,000 in park donations listed for 2027 is insufficient and that tax dollars should support parks improvements. Staff noted a potential $1.7 million general obligation borrowing target and discussed strategic timing of debt issuance to defer tax impact. No formal vote was taken; the presentation was informational ahead of the fall budget process.

### Green Tree Subdivision Update
Administrator Jim reported that the developer of the Green Tree subdivision indicated they expect to move forward within the next month and wants board action. The Plan Commission made a conditional recommendation in June, and staff noted some ordinance timing provisions require the board to act before the approval becomes automatic. A related factor is a pending decision regarding the golf course that may affect the subdivision layout. No vote was taken; the board noted this will return as a formal agenda item.

### Closed Session and Return - Police Union Negotiations
The board voted to convene in closed session to deliberate on the collective bargaining agreement with the Kronenwetter Professional Police Association / Wisconsin Professional Police Association. Upon returning to open session, the board voted to direct the administrator to continue negotiations with the union.
