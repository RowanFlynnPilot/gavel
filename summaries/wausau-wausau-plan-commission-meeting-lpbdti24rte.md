## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Plan Commission met on August 18th, 2026, approving several land use items including a rezoning on South 2nd Avenue, a transportation project plat for Highway 52, a conditional use permit for an assisted living facility on Mackendrick Street, and a blade sign approval. The commission also held a preliminary discussion on the previously adopted data center zoning ordinance, following a request from Common Council to keep the matter under periodic review.

## Key Discussions
### Public Comment on Agenda Items
The chair asked if anyone wished to offer public comment on non-hearing agenda items. No public comment was offered on non-hearing items, as those present indicated they were there for the public hearing.

### Minutes from July 21st, 2026
A motion was made by Paulkin and seconded by Barneman to accept the minutes. The commission approved them unanimously on a voice vote with no discussion.

### 3A: Conditional Use Permit – 1509 East Wausau Avenue (Patrick Meyer)
Patrick Meyer, a Vietnam veteran, appeared to seek a conditional use permit allowing more than one detached accessory building on his 0.7-acre TF10-zoned lot. Staff explained that a 12x24 structure qualifies as a garage under municipal ordinance and requires permitting; Meyer stated he was unaware a permit was needed for a skid-mounted shed. Supporters Jeff Traska and neighbor Carla Wescott testified in favor, praising Meyer's well-kept property and noting no neighboring objections. One commission member noted Meyer was the resident who posted a sign comparing local roads unfavorably to Vietnam, prompting a light moment about the Highway 52 project on the agenda. The hearing was closed and the item was continued to the next meeting for a decision.

### 3B / 4A: Rezoning – 117 and 121 South 2nd Avenue (Christine Pierce / Zivel Fabric LLC)
Christine Pierce appeared seeking a rezoning from urban mixed-use to neighborhood mixed-use zoning to enable a future lot split of two already-distinct buildings at 117 and 121 South 2nd Avenue. Staff noted the current zoning requires a 10,000 sq ft minimum lot, which the combined parcel falls just short of for a two-parcel split, and that the neighborhood mixed-use designation already applies across the street. No one opposed the rezoning at the public hearing. The commission then voted to approve the rezoning on a voice vote with no dissent.

### State Highway 52 Transportation Project Plat (TPP, Project ID 6999-00-01)
Staff member Eric Limon briefed the commission on the transportation project plat for the Highway 52 East Wausau Avenue corridor from North 6th Street to North 18th Avenue, explaining it identifies properties subject to potential temporary limited easements or permanent purchase. Approval by the commission would advance the plat to City Council, after which a real estate agent would contact affected property owners. No substantive debate occurred and the commission approved the plat unanimously on a voice vote.

### Conditional Use Permit – 151 Mackendrick Street (Beacon Resources LLC, Assisted Living Facility)
The commission considered a conditional use permit to allow construction of an assisted living facility in the downtown periphery mixed-use zoning district. Representatives from Beacon Resources LLC were present. No questions were raised by commissioners and the permit was approved unanimously on a voice vote.

### Blade Sign – 525 North 3rd Street (Finishing Touch Signs LLC)
The commission reviewed a proposed blade sign for 525 North 3rd Street in the downtown historic mixed-use zoning district. Commissioner Brugman raised a procedural suggestion that staff be empowered to approve blade signs administratively in the future to reduce commission workload, drawing laughter and informal support. Staff confirmed the sign met size and setback requirements but noted height clearance still needed field verification by the installing company. The sign was approved unanimously.

### Preliminary Code Amendment Discussion – Data Centers
Staff reported that the Common Council adopted the data center zoning ordinance at its July 14th meeting with one modification: a 200-foot distance buffer from residential properties. Staff stated they are satisfied with the ordinance as adopted and have fielded calls from other municipalities seeking guidance on the language. Commissioner Balkan expressed procedural concern about the Council modifying and then returning the ordinance to the Plan Commission. The City Attorney clarified that Council did not formally refer the matter back; rather, it was placed on the commission agenda in response to Alder Trueblood's informal request to keep the topic under review. Alderwoman Watson and others agreed periodic check-ins — perhaps annual or biannual — rather than monthly discussion would be appropriate, and no amendments were proposed.

## Public Comment
Three speakers offered comment during the public hearing for 1509 East Wausau Avenue. Patrick Meyer (1509 East Wausau Avenue) explained he was unaware a conditional use permit was required for his outbuildings. Jeff Traska (157129 Landart Road) spoke in support of Meyer, describing the emotional toll the permit process had on the Vietnam veteran and noting the neighborhood has other unpermitted structures. Carla Wescott (1312 Sherwood Circle), representing owners of adjacent property at 1505 East Park Avenue, testified that she had no objection to Meyer's buildings and called them well-kept, while urging city inspectors to look into other code concerns farther east on Park Avenue, including a tree service company parking a truck on the street. No public comment was offered on any other agenda item.

## Action Items

- Rezoning of 117 and 121 South 2nd Avenue approved; no further action noted
