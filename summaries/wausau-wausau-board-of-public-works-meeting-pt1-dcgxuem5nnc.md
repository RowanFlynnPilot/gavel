## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Board of Public Works held its regular meeting on July 14th, approving a series of contractor payments and change orders for ongoing construction and infrastructure projects across the city. All items passed unanimously, including payments for street construction, sidewalk repair, asphalt patching, water treatment plant demolition, and an amendment for parking ramp consulting work. The meeting concluded with a motion to enter closed session to deliberate on pre-qualification statements for the 2026 City Hall and fire department concrete project.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of July 8th Minutes
A motion to approve the minutes was made and seconded with no discussion. The motion passed unanimously.

### 2024 Street Construction Project B, Eau Claire Boulevard — Haas Sons Inc. Final Payment
Staff presented this item as a closeout of the 2024 Project B, releasing retainage of $10,000 to Haas Sons Incorporated. Staff recommended approval, the motion was made and seconded, and it passed unanimously.

### 2025 Water Treatment Plant Demo — MRD Group Pay Estimate No. 5
Staff noted the project is moving along and recommended approval of pay estimate number five in the amount of $92,150. The motion was made, seconded, and passed unanimously with no additional discussion.

### 2025 Sidewalk Repair Project A — SD Allenbecker Final Payment ($5,000)
A board member raised a question about whether this was the project that had a negative budget balance discussed at the prior meeting. Staff clarified that additional work was added but the overall budget was not exceeded. After the clarification, the board voted unanimously to approve the final payment of $5,000.

### 2026 Asphalt Patching Project — Zilisch Asphalt Pay Estimate No. 2
A discrepancy arose when the presiding officer read the payment amount as $19,318.65, which differed from the $17,568.18 shown in board members' packets. Staff explained the amount had been adjusted due to retainage subtraction and additional quantities, and the packet had not been updated. The motion was revised to approve $17,568.18 and passed unanimously.

### 2026 Street Construction Project A, Stark Street — Switlick & Sons Change Order No. 2
Staff explained the change order covers catch basin riser ring repairs just outside the project limits. The existing rings were completely degraded, so 12-inch risers and Crete Tech rings were added to avoid future repairs. The board approved the change order in the amount of $2,250 unanimously.

### 2026 Street Construction Project A, Stark Street — Switlick & Sons Pay Estimate No. 3
Staff recommended approval of pay estimate number three in the amount of $314,284.41. The motion was made, seconded, and passed unanimously with no additional discussion.

### 2026 Street Construction Project B, North 8th Avenue — Switlick & Sons Pay Estimate No. 3
Staff noted this payment covers work done at the intersection of Bridge Street and 8th Avenue, recommending approval in the amount of $23,589.24. The motion passed unanimously.

### Amendment to Agreement with Walker Consultants — Jefferson Street Parking Ramp
Staff explained that Walker Consultants, who designed and are providing construction administration for the Jefferson Street Parking Ramp project, requested additional hours due to changes in how the EIFS exterior finish is being applied, as the construction project ran from last year into this year. Staff noted the $6,550 request was reasonable and that Walker would bill only for actual hours used. The board approved the amendment unanimously.

### Closed Session — Pre-qualification Statements for 2026 City Hall and Fire Department Concrete Project
A motion was made to enter closed session pursuant to Wisconsin Statute 19.85(1)(e) to consider and deliberate on pre-qualification statements for the 2026 City Hall and fire department concrete project. The motion was seconded and passed unanimously, ending the public portion of the meeting.

## Public Comment
No public comment was offered.
