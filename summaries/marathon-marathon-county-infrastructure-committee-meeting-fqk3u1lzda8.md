# Marathon County Infrastructure Committee Meeting

**Organization:** Marathon County

## Meeting Overview
The Marathon County Infrastructure Committee approved a letter of support for Serenity/Wittenberg Tel's broadband expansion grant application and approved a 50/50 cost-share arrangement for upgrading County Highway F to eliminate seasonal weight limits for a local farm. The committee also received informational updates on the County H XX/Tesch Lane intersection safety study, 2027 budget development process, year-to-date highway fund financials, and IT department projects.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of June 4th Meeting Minutes
Motion was made by Gall and seconded by Coyle. No discussion occurred. The motion carried on a voice vote with no opposition.

### Letter of Support for Serenity/Wittenberg Tel Broadband Expansion Grant
Scott Nyman of Wittenberg Tel/Serenity presented to the committee, explaining that the state announced a $60 million broadband grant round that caught the industry by surprise. He described plans to submit two grant applications covering approximately 250 locations west of Pike Lake in the Town of Reed and south of Highway 29 in the Town of Ringle, with applications due July 27. Supervisor Robinson noted Serenity has been a consistent and reliable partner since 2018, and clarified the request is for a letter of support only, not financial contribution. Robinson also noted that Bug Tussle and Charter were encouraged to apply but Charter indicated corporate was still reviewing it. Motion by Coyle, seconded by Gall; approved unanimously on voice vote.

### County H XX and Tesch Lane/Terrebonne Intersection
Highway Commissioner Kevin Lane briefed the committee that after road work on County Trunk Highway XX was completed, a four-way stop that had been installed temporarily during construction was kept in place after crews observed drivers continuing to treat it as a four-way stop even after signs were removed. An engineering study and traffic count are underway, with results expected by the next meeting. Lane noted preliminary crash data shows approximately six crashes in a 12-month period, likely meeting the warrant for a permanent four-way stop. Village of Rothschild initially voted to revert to a two-way stop, though that decision predated the crash and traffic data. Village of Kronenwetter held a public meeting and was expected to favor keeping the four-way stop. No action was taken; the item is informational pending the engineering study.

### County Highway F Removal of Seasonal Weight Limits
Lane presented two cost scenarios for upgrading a segment of County Highway F to eliminate seasonal weight limits at the request of a local farm operator (Mr. Gums): a combination aggregate/asphalt option at approximately $30,000, or an all-asphalt option at approximately $42,000. Past practice has been a 50/50 county-farmer cost share, putting the farmer's share at approximately $15,000, but the farmer countered with an offer of $8,000. The committee moved forward at the 50/50 split ($15,000 farmer share) based on past practice. Motion by Gault, seconded by Coyle; approved unanimously on voice vote. Chair Dickinson noted the committee should develop a formal written policy for such requests.

### 2027 Budget Development Discussion
County Administrator Linder and Committee Chair Robinson led a discussion framing the 2027 budget process around three key questions: what programs/services to deliver, at what service level, and at what cost. Linder highlighted that the mandatory/discretionary program document and the comprehensive fee schedule are in the packet for committee review. He noted potential future program discussions such as a countywide community care paramedic program and a carbon credit program for county forestry. Robinson and Vice Chair Galbreath discussed general transportation aids (GTA), noting the county received $3.163 million in GTA for 2026, a notable increase, and that maintaining expenditure levels is critical to sustaining future aid. Linder announced the county received its first Government Finance Officers Association budget award in many years. No action was taken; the item will remain on future agendas through budget adoption.

### Year-to-Date Budget Report
Finance staff presented the highway fund (Fund 610) budget report through March 2026, noting expenditures at 19.86% of the annual budget, slightly below the 22.75% benchmark, which was described as on track given the additional winter storm costs during the period. Revenues are also on track and interest income is performing at 48.14% of budget. Supervisor Gall confirmed that leftover revenues remain within the fund. No action was taken.

### CCIT (IT Department) Project Updates
IT Director Jerry briefed the committee on current projects including completing a phone system upgrade (only North Central Health Care remains), mobile device management rollout, a critical upgrade to the Genetec camera system supporting the jail's intercom system, AI/Co-pilot training rollout, and numerous software platform transitions at the Medical Examiner's office, Parks Department, and Sheriff's Department. He noted the department submitted a large packet for the Sheriff's Department CJIS audit by the Department of Justice and expects the resulting report to generate significant security-related work. Two positions are currently open for recruitment and one new staff member was recently onboarded. No action was taken.

## Public Comment
No public comment was offered. No one had signed up for public comment.

## Action Items
- Committee approved providing a letter of county support to Serenity/Wittenberg Tel for the state broadband expansion grant application, to be submitted July 24
- Committee approved proceeding with County Highway F seasonal weight limit removal upgrade at a 50/50 cost share (approximately $15,000 from the requesting farm operator)
- Engineering study on the County H XX/Tesch Lane/Terrebonne intersection to be completed and presented at the August 6 meeting
- Chair Dickinson directed staff to develop a formal written policy for cost-sharing on road upgrades requested by commercial or agricultural entities
- Budget development discussion to continue at future meetings through adoption; committee members asked to bring program, service level, and fee input to staff
- Supervisor Gogga requested a future agenda item discussing the county's projects in the Metropolitan Planning Organization Transportation Improvement Program (TIP)
- Next Infrastructure Committee meeting scheduled for Thursday, August 6, 2026 at 9:00 a.m.
