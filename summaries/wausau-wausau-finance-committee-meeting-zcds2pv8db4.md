## Meeting Overview
The Wausau Finance Committee met on August 12, 2026 and approved five action items in a brief session, including hiring an insurance consultant to competitively bid the city's property and casualty coverage for the first time since 2007, two sole-source equipment purchases for Public Works and the Municipal Airport, and two bond issuances totaling nearly $6.8 million to fund the 2026 lead service line replacement program. The meeting was significant for residents as the bond financing at a 0.25% interest rate — with a forgivable portion — represents a favorable deal for both the water utility and property owners with private lateral replacements.

## Key Discussions
### Approval of July 28, 2026 Finance Committee Minutes
A committee member moved to approve the minutes as recorded, with Henke providing the second. No discussion was held and the motion carried on a voice vote.

### Gander Consulting Group — Insurance Consulting Services for 2027 Renewal
Finance staff (identified as Mary Ann) explained the city has been with SIVIC since 2007 without competitive review. She noted Gander Consulting would issue an RFP, review proposals, and recommend the best deal while accounting for ancillary services SIVIC provides, including NeoGov learning used by HR at no cost. The likely competitor would be the League policy. Staff recommended keeping MPIC for property insurance since the city switched to them only two years ago. A committee member noted '20 years is a long time to be with a single carrier.' The motion to authorize the engagement carried unanimously on a voice vote, moved by Neil and seconded by Henke.

### Sole Source — Stacking Conveyor from Kafka Conveyors and Equipment Inc.
Solomon from Public Works confirmed the conveyor is at end of life, heavily corroded from years of stacking salt, and that Kafka holds the original design plans, making sole source appropriate and cost-effective. Finance director Mary Ann concurred with the sole source justification. Alder Neale raised the question of written justification to the finance director, which was addressed. The motion carried unanimously, moved by Neale and seconded by Henke.

### Sole Source — Jet A Refueler from Wheels/Phillips 66 for Wausau Municipal Airport
Solomon explained the current refueler dates to 1996 and that purchasing this used unit conforms to Phillips 66 contract requirements with Wausau Flying Service and saves significant money over new procurement. He noted bids were obtained from Garsite, Westmore, and Skymark through Phillips 66 but all came in significantly higher. A committee member asked clarifying questions about the sole source justification and expressed satisfaction with the due diligence. The chair noted the prior arrangement involved the city reimbursing Wausau Flying Service and Phillips 66, making direct ownership cleaner. The motion carried unanimously, moved by Neale and seconded by [inaudible].

### Water System Revenue Bond Series 2026 — Up to $3,469,102 (Lead Service Line — Utility Side)
Mary Ann explained this 0.25% fixed-rate Safe Drinking Water loan with a forgivable portion funds the utility side of the 2026 lead service line replacement program, with Five Star already performing work under a previously authorized contract. A committee member asked about the impact on water rates, and staff indicated Ehlers will return to the commission in September with a financial analysis; a rate case may be forthcoming. Total water utility debt was estimated at approximately $145 million as of December 31, 2025. Staff noted the loan is drawn down like a construction loan rather than paid out in a lump sum, with the DNR reviewing each draw for eligibility. The motion carried, moved by Henke and seconded by Neale.

### General Obligation Promissory Notes Series 2026B — Up to $3,331,289 (Lead Service Line — Private Side)
This taxable GO note covers the private lateral replacement side of the same lead service line program at 0.25% interest, structured as a 10-year note resulting in approximately $340,000 in annual repayments. Staff confirmed the city expects to draw close to the full amount with some contingency. Mary Ann flagged that 2027 lead service line funding applications are already submitted and a similar financing gap will need to come before the committee. The motion carried, moved by Henke and seconded by Neale.

## Public Comment
No public comment was offered.

## Action Items
- Authorize engagement of Gander Consulting Group to conduct RFP process for 2027 property and casualty insurance renewal, excluding MPIC property coverage
- Approve sole source purchase of stacking conveyor from Kafka Conveyors and Equipment Inc. for the Department of Public Works
- Approve sole source purchase of used Jet A refueler from Wheels/Phillips 66 for Wausau Municipal Airport
- Authorize issuance and sale of up to $3,469,102 Water System Revenue Bond Series 2026 for lead service line replacement (utility side); forward to full Council
- Authorize issuance and sale of up to $3,331,289 Taxable General Obligation Promissory Notes Series 2026B for lead service line replacement (private side); forward to full Council
- Staff to prepare 2027 lead service line financing gap analysis for future Finance Committee consideration
