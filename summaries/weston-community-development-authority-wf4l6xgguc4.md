## Meeting Overview
The Village of Weston Community Development Authority held a brief meeting on the closing of TIF District No. 1, which added $354 million in assessed value since 1998 and accumulated $95.7 million in increment over its life. The CDA voted unanimously to redeem the outstanding 2017A lease revenue bonds early, paying off debt ahead of the 2031 schedule. The board also received an update on TIF District No. 2, which carries a $841,000 deficit and is expected to close within a few years, and discussed the future role of the CDA itself.

## Key Discussions
### Minutes of Previous Meeting – March 10, 2020
A motion to approve the minutes was made by Lisa and seconded by Ted. The motion passed unanimously with no discussion.

### Update on TIF District No. 1
Staff (identified as Jessica) reported that TIF District No. 1, open since 1998, has recovered all costs and is ready to close. The TIF added $354 million in assessed value, incurred $86 million in project costs, and collected $95.7 million in total increment. Members asked whether sufficient funds exist to call the outstanding debt; Jessica confirmed they do. Staff noted that closing the TIF will shift the assessed value to the general tax roll, likely producing a significant tax rate reduction for village residents, and that the village expects to be allowed to increase its levy by approximately $400,000. Approximately $200,000 in administration cost allocations will also return to the general fund.

### Update on TIF District No. 2
Jessica reported that TIF District No. 2, created in 2004 around Schofield Avenue, carries a $841,000 deficit following a large state-mandated value reduction last year. The TIF has no remaining debt, only the deficit to repay through ongoing increment. No new projects or expenditures are planned; the district is expected to close before its 2031 deadline. Members also briefly discussed the possibility of future TIFs, including a potential small district near Highway J, but staff noted no formal proposal exists and that changes to state TIF law are still evolving.

### Approval of Resolution Authorizing Redemption of CDA Lease Revenue Bonds Series 2017A
The board voted on a resolution to call and redeem the outstanding CDA lease revenue bonds (Series 2017A, dated July 13, 2017), paying bondholders in full with interest rather than continuing payments through 2031. A member identified as Hogan moved approval and Mark seconded. The motion passed unanimously.

### Future Role of the CDA
Members and staff discussed whether the CDA should continue to exist once TIF 2 closes, noting it has no debt to administer and has not conducted business visits since 2018. Staff suggested bringing the question of disbanding or repurposing the CDA to the full Village Board for direction, and a member asked staff to add it to the board's next monthly agenda. Members noted the CDA is established by ordinance, so any dissolution would require a formal ordinance change.

## Public Comment
No public comment was offered.

## Action Items

- Staff to place the question of the CDA's future role and potential disbandment on the Village Board's next monthly agenda
- Staff to monitor TIF District No. 2 deficit repayment and report progress toward closure
- Final revenue numbers for TIF District No. 1 closure (including remaining Weston Avenue increment) to be compiled
