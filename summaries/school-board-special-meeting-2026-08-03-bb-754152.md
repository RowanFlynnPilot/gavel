## Meeting Overview
The Wausau School Board held a special meeting on August 3, 2026, approving a consent agenda, a ratified teacher salary agreement with the WEA (0.714% schedule increase within the board's 2.63% parameter), and employee handbook changes including expanded longevity bonuses and a new flexible sick-to-personal-day conversion policy. Following the short special meeting, the board transitioned to a separate special meeting workshop covering a school perception survey, strategic plan, and potential referendum discussions.

## Key Discussions
### Approve Consent Agenda
The board moved quickly to approve the consent agenda covering appointments, additional and replacement staff, contract increases and decreases, separations, resignations, terminations, leaves of absence, and retirements. No discussion was recorded. The motion passed unanimously.

### Teacher Salary Approval for 2026-2027
A presenter (identified as Ms. Gundrum or Ms. Channel) reported that the WEA had ratified a settlement the prior week providing a 0.714% increase on the salary schedule, within the board's established parameter of 2.63%. She noted 11 staff members would receive two-step movement for earning master's degrees, and no staff earned new national board certification this year. A board member asked about competitive positioning relative to other districts; administration responded that recruitment has not been significantly hindered, with only one or two candidates per year lost due to salary expectations, and that neighboring districts made similar increases. The motion to accept the certified staff salary changes passed unanimously.

### Employee Handbook Updates for 2026-27
Ms. Gundrum presented two proposed handbook changes for teaching staff. First, the longevity bonus stipend of $500 (paid at 5, 10, 15, and 20 years of experience, established last year) would be extended to cover years 25, 30, 35, and 40. Second, a new provision would allow teachers to convert sick leave days into personal leave days on an escalating scale tied to years of service: 1 day at 5 years, 2 at 10, 3 at 15, 4 at 20, and 5 at 25 years. A board member raised the question of whether the longevity bonus should extend beyond 40 years to cover any employee who might work 45 or more years, citing unlikely but possible scenarios. Administration acknowledged the suggestion but noted no current teacher has reached that threshold. The motion to approve the handbook language changes passed unanimously.

### Special Meeting Workshop (School Perception Survey, Strategic Plan, Potential Referendum)
After adjourning the special meeting, Board President Bouché called the special meeting workshop to order, noting that Superintendent Bushman and administration would present on a school perception survey, strategic plan, and initial referendum discussions. The board relocated to the PDC (Professional Development Center) in the educational wing for a workshop-style setup. No votes or detailed discussion from that session were captured in the transcript.
